## Brand & Style

This design system is crafted for a premier commercial poultry and sustainable agriculture enterprise operating across Ogun and Lagos, Nigeria. The design merges the earthy authority of high-yield institutional farming with the precision and cleanliness of modern agri-tech commerce. 

The aesthetic is **Corporate / Modern** infused with an organic, sunlit agricultural warmth. It rejects the stereotypical "dusty" farm aesthetic in favor of industrial efficiency, biosecurity standards, and modern consumer trust:
- **Clean & Trustworthy:** Crisp slate borders, structured white space, and clear typography demonstrate operational rigor and food safety compliance.
- **Vibrant & Grounded:** Lush deep canopy greens anchored by harvest amber sun-burst accents mirror healthy crops, fertile land, and poultry productivity.
- **Institutional Clarity:** Precision data displays, legible inventory cards, and structured forms cater equally to institutional B2B bulk buyers, retail distributors, and farm operations personnel.

## Colors

The palette directly references lush field furrows and golden harvest sunlight, balanced against neutral slate tones for enterprise legibility:

- **Primary (`#0e3e1e`):** Deep Forest Green. Used for core brand headers, primary call-to-action buttons, high-level navigation, and prominent structural frames. Represents stability, biosecurity, and ecological stewardship.
- **Secondary (`#f59e0b`):** Harvest Amber / Warm Gold. Applied deliberately for high-priority alerts, key metrics, promotional badges, active selection states, and primary accents that evoke sunrise and vitality.
- **Tertiary (`#15803d`):** Fresh Canopy Green. Serves as positive feedback, success state indicators, yield growth charts, and fresh produce status indicators.
- **Neutrals:**
  - Base Dark: `#0f172a` (Slate 900) for primary text and high-contrast iconography.
  - Body Slate: `#334155` (Slate 700) for body copy, secondary metadata, and table column headers.
  - Subtle Border: `#e2e8f0` (Slate 200) for clean card dividers and form input outlines.
  - Soft Field Tint: `#f0fdf4` (Emerald 50) and `#f8fafc` (Slate 50) for alternate row striping, subtle card surfaces, and section backgrounds.

## Typography

The type system blends the energetic curves of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for brand headlines and interface tags with the technical legibility of **Inter** for dense tabular data, batch tracking, and descriptive editorial.

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans (600 and 700 weights) with tight tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`). Gives announcements, batch labels, and farm unit headers a grounded yet forward-looking character.
- **Body:** Inter handles all administrative, operational, and narrative passages. Its tall x-height ensures readability in variable field conditions and outdoor mobile viewing.
- **Labels & Badges:** Plus Jakarta Sans at small sizes with uppercase or title-case configurations provides clear wayfinding across batch stages (e.g., "BROODER A", "INSPECTION PASSED", "OUT OF STOCK").

## Layout & Spacing

A strict 8pt grid governs spatial relations across screens, scaling down to 4pt increments for dense data dashboards.

- **Grid Systems:**
  - **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and up to `3rem` outer margins. Content maximum width is capped at 1280px for standard pages and 1440px for operations dashboards.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` page margins.
  - **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` gutters, and `1rem` safe-area page margins.
- **Reflow & Rhythm:**
  - Administrative data tables collapse into stacked item cards on mobile screens with explicit status headers.
  - Media galleries transition from 4 columns on desktop to 2 columns on tablet, and single-column carousels on mobile.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal layers** combined with subtle, warm-tinted **ambient shadows** to emulate sunlight hitting tactile surfaces.

- **Layer 0 (Base Canvas):** Light field background (`#f8fafc` or pure `#ffffff`) provides the crisp baseline.
- **Layer 1 (Cards & Data Panels):** Pure `#ffffff` resting on Layer 0, bounded by a 1px ghost border (`#e2e8f0`). Elevated with a soft ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Layer 2 (Dropdowns, Interactive Popovers & Hovered Cards):** Lifted with: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(14, 62, 30, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Sticky Navigation Bar):** Semi-opaque backdrop (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)`) paired with high-definition elevation: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(14, 62, 30, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(14, 62, 30, 0.06)`.
- **Accent Highlight:** Featured items or active states use an amber rim glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded** shape language (`roundedness: 2` = 0.5rem / 8px base radius). 

- **Base Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** Standardized at `0.5rem` (8px). This creates an approachable, ergonomic feel without compromising corporate authority.
- **Cards, Modals & Data Containers (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`):** Configured between `0.75rem` (12px) and `1rem` (16px) to enclose poultry batch metrics, farm photography, and operational forms cleanly.
- **Status Pills & Metric Chips:** Fully circular/pill-shaped (`9999px`) to maintain clear visual contrast against rectangular inputs and data tables.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Forest Green (`#0e3e1e`) background, white text, 8px corner radius. Hover shifts to `#14532d` with a subtle elevation lift. Active state compresses slightly (`scale: 0.98`).
- **Secondary / Action Accent:** Harvest Amber (`#f59e0b`) background, `#0f172a` slate text, designed for commercial actions ("Place Bulk Order", "Request Farm Tour").
- **Outline:** Transparent background, 1.5px border in `#0e3e1e`, text in `#0e3e1e`. Hover fills with `#f0fdf4`.
- **Ghost:** Unbordered, text in `#334155`, hover fills with `#f1f5f9`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Batch / Category Chips:** Pill-shaped with subtle tinted backgrounds. For example, "Poultry": background `#ecfdf5`, text `#065f46`, border 1px `#a7f3d0`.
- **Status Indicators:** 
  - *Active / Ready for Dispatch:* Tertiary green fill (`#dcfce7`), text `#15803d`, leading 6px pulsing dot.
  - *Incubation / In Progress:* Amber fill (`#fef3c7`), text `#b45309`.
  - *Sold Out / Quarantined:* Slate fill (`#f1f5f9`), text `#64748b`.

### Input Fields & Forms
- **Base Style:** 8px border radius, 1px border `#cbd5e1`, background `#ffffff`, text `#0f172a`, padding `0.625rem 0.875rem`.
- **Focus State:** Border changes to `#0e3e1e`, accompanied by a 3px ring in `rgba(14, 62, 30, 0.15)`.
- **Error State:** Border changes to `#ef4444`, text `#991b1b`, error message with icon directly below input.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 20px size with 4px radius (checkboxes) and circular form (radio).
- Unchecked: `#cbd5e1` border against white.
- Checked: `#0e3e1e` solid fill with crisp white checkmark/dot. Focus ring in amber `#f59e0b`.

### Cards & Media Gallery
- **Product & Lot Cards:** Enclosed in 12px rounded borders with 1px `#e2e8f0`. Images feature a 16:10 or 4:3 aspect ratio with subtle zoom on hover (`scale: 1.03`, duration 300ms). Footer houses batch size, price in NGN (₦), and direct ordering CTA.
- **Media Gallery Grids:** Clean masonry or uniform grid with 16px gaps, rounded 12px corners, overlay badges showing farm facility location (e.g., "Abeokuta Layer Facility", "Epe Feed Mill").

### Data Tables
- Header row styled in `#f8fafc` with uppercase `label-md` in `#475569`.
- Rows bordered with 1px bottom divider `#f1f5f9`. Row hover shifts background to `#f0fdf4`.
- Numerical columns (quantities, weights, prices) are right-aligned using tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

### Navigation & Header
- **Desktop Navbar:** Sticky white bar with a 1px border-bottom (`#e2e8f0`). Logo on the left, centered navigation links in `#334155` (hovering to `#0e3e1e`), and right-hand action button cluster in Harvest Amber.
- **Sub-navigation / Facility Switcher:** Pill-shaped tab list with active indicator highlighted in Deep Forest Green with white text.
